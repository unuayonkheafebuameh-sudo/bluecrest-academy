About BlueCrest International Academy

A co-educational day school offering Primary, Junior Secondary (JSS 1–3) and Senior Secondary (SSS 1–3) education.

Our vision

To raise confident, ethical and globally competitive learners who lead with knowledge and integrity.

Our mission

To deliver excellent teaching, a safe community and modern learning tools that bring out each child's best.

Why choose BlueCrest?

Small classes

Personal attention for every learner.

Digital campus

Results, fees and timetables online.

Tracked routes with trained drivers.

Whole-child growth

Sports, arts, clubs and leadership.

Academic programmes

Primary 1–6

Literacy, numeracy, science and creative arts built on the NERDC basic education curriculum.

Junior Secondary

JSS 1–3 with Basic Science, Basic Technology, Business Studies and preparation for the BECE.

Senior Secondary

Science, Arts and Commercial tracks preparing students for WAEC and NECO.
